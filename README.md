## What I'm Building

### Algoritic Inc.

**SaaS & Software Solutions for African Businesses**

Algoritic is my primary venture, focused on building technology that helps African SMEs solve operational and financial problems.

Our flagship product is **Monietar**, an AI-powered cash-flow management platform designed to help businesses understand their sales, profits, expenses, and cash position.

We also work on custom software development, digital products, and technology consulting.

**Website:** [algoritic.com.ng](https://algoritic.com.ng)

---

### Krilarr

**The developer portfolio, proof & verification platform.**

Krilarr is being built around a simple idea:

> **A developer's portfolio should show proof, not just claims.**

The platform helps developers showcase their work, experience, projects, and evidence of what they can actually build.

Krilarr has been launched across platforms including Product Hunt, Peerlist, and SaaS Hive and is currently focused on product validation, growth, and go-to-market.

**Status:** Pre-seed · Building in public
**Website:** [krilarr.vercel.app](https://krilarr.vercel.app)
